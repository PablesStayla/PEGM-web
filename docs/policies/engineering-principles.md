## 🌟 Core Philosophy
 
At PranaKaia, we believe that software is a liability, not an asset. Our goal is to maintain the smallest, highest-quality code surface possible while delivering maximum business value. We adhere to the **Absolute Zero** standard: zero technical debt, zero silent failures, and zero ambiguity.
 
---
 
## 🏛️ The Five Pillars
 
### 1. Ownership & Sovereignty
- **You build it, you run it.** Every engineer is an owner of the system's success.
- **Structural Sovereignty:** We prioritize architectural integrity over short-term features.
 
### 2. Type Hardening (Total Safety)
- **Absolute Zero Type Drift:** We do not tolerate `any` or unsafely casted types.
- **Schema-First:** Our data structures are the source of truth for our code.
 
### 3. Operational Excellence (Observability First)
- **Telemetry as a Feature:** Features are not "done" until they are observable.
- **Evidence-Based Progress:** We measure success through metrics (SLIs/SLOs), not narratives.
 
### 4. Strategic Simplicity
- **Modular Monolith:** We prefer bounded contexts within a unified system over distributed complexity.
- **Minimal Dependencies:** Every third-party library is a security and maintenance risk.
 
### 5. Defensive & Predictable Engineering
- **Railway-Oriented Programming:** We use `Result` patterns instead of exceptions for flow control.
- **Deterministic Environments:** From local dev to production, the environment must be identical.
 
---
 
## 🚦 Decision Framework
 
When faced with a trade-off, prioritize in this order:
1. **Security & Reliability** (Zero Data Loss)
2. **Maintainability** (Clean Architecture)
3. **Performance** (Core Web Vitals)
4. **Developer Velocity** (Tooling)
