# Standard: Engineering Communication
 
> **🏆 ABSOLUTE ZERO - Synchronous & Asynchronous Sovereignty**
>
> *Version: 5.0 | Status: 🔒 Institutional Governance | Objective: Zero Noise, Maximum Signal*
 
---
 
## 📋 Communication Philosophy
 
We prioritize **Asynchronous Communication** to protect our engineers' "Deep Work" time. Meetings are a last resort, used only for complex debate or team bonding.
 
---
 
## 💬 Slack Etiquette (The "Signal" Rule)
 
- **Channel First**: Prefer public channels over DMs to ensure knowledge is searchable and accessible.
- **Thread Culture**: Always reply in threads to keep the main channel clean.
- **Urgency**: Use `@here` or `@channel` only for P0/P1 incidents.
- **Status**: Use statuses (🎧, 🧪, 🛌) to signal availability.
 
---
 
## 📝 Documentation First
 
Before starting a major feature or refactor, you must follow the **RFC/ADR** process.
- **RFC (Request for Comments)**: A proposal to solicit feedback.
- **ADR (Architecture Decision Record)**: A finalized decision that is committed to the repository in `docs/adr/`.
 
---
 
## 🐙 GitHub & Pull Requests
 
The Pull Request is our most important communication artifact.
- **Context**: Every PR must include a clear description of the "Why" and "How."
- **Review Speed**: Reviewers should aim to provide feedback within 4 business hours.
- **Conventional Commits**: Mandatory for all messages (`feat:`, `fix:`, `refactor:`, `docs:`).
