## Purpose & Scope / Propósito y Alcance {#purpose--scope}

### English
This Code of Conduct establishes the standards of behavior expected from all individuals who participate in the PranaKaia E-Commerce Platform project, including employees, contractors, contributors, maintainers, and community members. It applies to all project spaces, including but not limited to:

- Source code repositories (GitHub, GitLab)
- Issue trackers and pull requests
- Documentation and wiki pages
- Communication channels (Slack, Discord, email, forums)
- Virtual and in-person meetings, conferences, and events
- Social media presence representing the project
- One-on-one communications related to the project

**Scope:** This code applies when an individual represents the project or community, including use of official email addresses, social media accounts, or acting as a designated representative at events.

### Español
Este Código de Conducta establece los estándares de comportamiento esperados de todas las personas que participan en el proyecto PranaKaia E-Commerce Platform, incluyendo empleados, contratistas, contribuidores, mantenedores y miembros de la comunidad. Aplica a todos los espacios del proyecto, incluyendo pero no limitado a:

- Repositorios de código fuente (GitHub, GitLab)
- Rastreadores de issues y pull requests
- Documentación y páginas wiki
- Canales de comunicación (Slack, Discord, email, foros)
- Reuniones virtuales y presenciales, conferencias y eventos
- Presencia en redes sociales representando el proyecto
- Comunicaciones uno-a-uno relacionadas con el proyecto

**Alcance:** Este código aplica cuando un individuo representa al proyecto o comunidad, incluyendo el uso de direcciones de correo electrónico oficiales, cuentas de redes sociales, o actuando como representante designado en eventos.

## Acceptable Behavior / Comportamiento Aceptable {#acceptable-behavior}

### English

The following behaviors are expected and encouraged from all community members:

#### Professional Conduct
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project
- Maintaining professionalism in all communications

#### Collaborative Engagement
- Actively listening to others and seeking to understand before responding
- Acknowledging contributions and giving credit where it is due
- Being patient with others who may be learning or less experienced
- Helping others succeed and grow in their roles
- Sharing knowledge freely and mentoring when appropriate

#### Inclusive Practices
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being mindful of cultural differences and language barriers
- Ensuring accessibility in documentation and communication
- Actively inviting participation from underrepresented groups
- Creating space for all voices to be heard

#### Technical Excellence
- Writing clean, maintainable, and well-documented code
- Following established coding standards and best practices
- Participating in code reviews with a constructive mindset
- Testing thoroughly and taking responsibility for code quality
- Documenting decisions and sharing knowledge

### Español

Los siguientes comportamientos son esperados y fomentados de todos los miembros de la comunidad:

#### Conducta Profesional

- Dar y aceptar con gracia retroalimentación constructiva
- Centrarse en lo que es mejor para la comunidad y el proyecto

## Unacceptable Behavior / Comportamiento Inaceptable {#unacceptable-behavior}

### English

The following behaviors are considered unacceptable and will not be tolerated:

#### Harassment & Discrimination
| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| **Verbal Harassment** | Insults, derogatory comments, personal attacks, threats |
| **Discrimination** | Unequal treatment based on protected characteristics |
| **Sexual Harassment** | Unwelcome sexual attention, advances, or comments |
| **Bullying** | Intimidation, coercion, or abuse of power |
| **Retaliation** | Punishing someone for reporting violations |

#### Unprofessional Conduct
- **Trolling:** Deliberately inflammatory or off-topic comments
- **Doxing:** Publishing private information without consent
- **Impersonation:** Misrepresenting identity or affiliation
- **Spam:** Unsolicited commercial or promotional content
- **Plagiarism:** Using others' work without attribution

#### Technical Misconduct
- Intentionally introducing security vulnerabilities or malicious code
- Sabotaging others' work or the project's infrastructure
- Unauthorized access to systems or data
- Violating intellectual property rights
- Sharing proprietary or confidential information

#### Communication Violations
- Sustained disruption of discussions or meetings
- Excessive or inappropriate use of @mentions or direct messages
- Ignoring communication preferences or boundaries
- Recording conversations without consent
- Creating hostile or intimidating environments

### Español

Los siguientes comportamientos se consideran inaceptables y no serán tolerados:

#### Acoso y Discriminación
| Categoría | Ejemplos |
|-----------|----------|
| **Acoso Verbal** | Insultos, comentarios despectivos, ataques personales, amenazas |
| **Discriminación** | Trato desigual basado en características protegidas |
| **Acoso Sexual** | Atención sexual no deseada, insinuaciones o comentarios |
| **Intimidación** | Intimidación, coerción o abuso de poder |
| **Represalia** | Castigar a alguien por reportar violaciones |

#### Conducta No Profesional
- **Trolling:** Comentarios deliberadamente inflamatorios o fuera de tema
- **Doxing:** Publicar información privada sin consentimiento
- **Suplantación:** Falsificar identidad o afiliación
- **Spam:** Contenido comercial o promocional no solicitado
- **Plagio:** Usar el trabajo de otros sin atribución

#### Conducta Técnica Incorrecta
- Introducir intencionalmente vulnerabilidades de seguridad o código malicioso
- Sabotear el trabajo de otros o la infraestructura del proyecto
- Acceso no autorizado a sistemas o datos
- Violación de derechos de propiedad intelectual
- Compartir información propietaria o confidencial

#### Violaciones de Comunicación
- Disrupción sostenida de discusiones o reuniones
- Uso excesivo o inapropiado de @menciones o mensajes directos
- Ignorar preferencias o límites de comunicación
- Grabar conversaciones sin consentimiento
- Crear ambientes hostiles o intimidantes

## Professional Code of Conduct / Código de Conducta Profesional {#professional-code-of-conduct}

### Enterprise Standards / Estándares Empresariales

#### 1. Confidentiality / Confidencialidad
- **English:** Protect sensitive information including API keys, credentials, customer data, and proprietary business information. Report any suspected data breaches immediately.
- **Español:** Proteja información sensible incluyendo claves API, credenciales, datos de clientes e información comercial propietaria. Reporte cualquier sospecha de violación de datos inmediatamente.

#### 2. Conflict of Interest / Conflicto de Intereses
- **English:** Disclose any personal, financial, or professional relationships that could create conflicts of interest.
- **Español:** Divulgue cualquier relación personal, financiera o profesional que pueda crear conflictos de intereses.

#### 3. Intellectual Property / Propiedad Intelectual
- **English:** Respect copyright, patents, trademarks, and trade secrets. Contribute only original work or properly licensed materials.
- **Español:** Respete derechos de autor, patentes, marcas registradas y secretos comerciales. Contribuya solo trabajo original o materiales con licencia apropiada.

#### 4. Compliance / Cumplimiento
- **English:** Adhere to applicable laws and regulations including GDPR, PCI-DSS, SOC2, and data protection requirements.
- **Español:** Cumpla con leyes y regulaciones aplicables incluyendo GDPR, PCI-DSS, SOC2 y requisitos de protección de datos.

#### 5. Security / Seguridad
- **English:** Follow security best practices. Report vulnerabilities through proper channels. Never exploit security weaknesses.
- **Español:** Siga mejores prácticas de seguridad. Reporte vulnerabilidades a través de canales apropiados. Nunca explote debilidades de seguridad.

#### 6. Communication / Comunicación
- **English:** Use clear, respectful language. Avoid profanity, discriminatory jokes, or offensive content.
- **Español:** Use lenguaje claro y respetuoso. Evite profanidad, chistes discriminatorios o contenido ofensivo.

4. **Resolution / Resolución:** Target resolution within 14 business days (may vary based on complexity).
5. **Follow-up / Seguimiento:** Outcome communicated to reporter (subject to privacy constraints).

## Investigation Process / Proceso de Investigación {#investigation-process}

### Investigation Principles / Principios de Investigación

| Principle / Principio | Description / Descripción |
|----------------------|---------------------------|
| **Fairness / Imparcialidad** | All parties are treated equally and without bias. / Todas las partes son tratadas igual y sin sesgo. |
| **Confidentiality / Confidencialidad** | Information is shared only on a need-to-know basis. / La información se comparte solo en base de necesidad. |
| **Promptness / Prontitud** | Investigations are conducted without unnecessary delay. / Las investigaciones se realizan sin demoras innecesarias. |
| **Thoroughness / Exhaustividad** | All relevant evidence is gathered and reviewed. / Se recopila y revisa toda la evidencia relevante. |
| **Transparency / Transparencia** | Parties are informed of the process and outcomes. / Las partes son informadas del proceso y resultados. |

### Investigation Stages / Etapas de Investigación

```
REPORT RECEIVED
      ↓
[0-24h] ACKNOWLEDGMENT
      ↓
[24-48h] INITIAL ASSESSMENT
   - Severity classification
   - Resource assignment
   - Preliminary review
      ↓
[48h-14d] FORMAL INVESTIGATION
   - Evidence collection
   - Witness interviews
   - Subject notification
   - Analysis
      ↓
INVESTIGATION COMPLETE
      ↓
DECISION & RECOMMENDATION
      ↓
ENFORCEMENT ACTION
      ↓
APPEALS WINDOW (7 days)
      ↓
CLOSURE & DOCUMENTATION
```

### Rights of Involved Parties / Derechos de las Partes Involucradadas

**English:**
- Right to know the nature of the allegations
- Right to respond to allegations
- Right to present evidence and witnesses
- Right to confidentiality
- Right to appeal decisions

**Español:**
- Derecho a conocer la naturaleza de las alegaciones
- Derecho a responder a las alegaciones
- Derecho a presentar evidencia y testigos
- Derecho a confidencialidad
- Derecho a apelar decisiones

## Enforcement Actions / Medidas de Aplicación {#enforcement-actions}

### Severity Levels / Niveles de Severidad

| Level | Examples | Response Time | Typical Actions |
|-------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Minor / Menor** | First-time etiquette violations | 7 days | Warning, coaching |
| **Moderate / Moderado** | Repeated violations, minor harassment | 3 days | Temporary restriction, mandatory training |
| **Serious / Grave** | Harassment, discrimination, security violations | 24 hours | Suspension, contract review |
| **Critical / Crítico** | Illegal activity, severe harassment, data breach | Immediate | Immediate removal, legal action |

### Enforcement Actions / Acciones de Aplicación

#### 1. Correction / Corrección
- **Private conversation** to clarify expectations
- **Warning** with documented expectations
- **Required training** on relevant topics

#### 2. Temporary Measures / Medidas Temporales
- **Temporary ban** from specific channels
- **Moderated participation** (all posts reviewed)
- **Cooling-off period** from interactions

#### 3. Permanent Measures / Medidas Permanentes
- **Removal** from maintainer status
- **Ban** from project spaces
- **Termination** of employment/contract

#### 4. Legal Actions / Acciones Legales
- **Cease and desist** notices
- **Law enforcement** referral
- **Civil litigation** for damages

### Decision Documentation / Documentación de Decisiones

All enforcement decisions are documented including:
- Incident summary
- Evidence reviewed
- Applicable code sections
- Decision rationale
- Appeal rights

## Appeals Process / Proceso de Apelación {#appeals-process}

### Grounds for Appeal / Motivos de Apelación

**English:** An appeal may be filed if:
- New evidence has become available
- The investigation process was flawed
- The decision was disproportionate to the violation
- There was a conflict of interest in the investigation

**Español:** Una apelación puede presentarse si:
- Nueva evidencia ha surgido
- El proceso de investigación fue defectuoso
- La decisión fue desproporcionada a la violación
- Hubo un conflicto de intereses en la investigación

### Appeal Procedure / Procedimiento de Apelación

1. **Filing / Presentación:** Submit appeal within 7 days of decision to `appeals@pranakaia.cl`
2. **Acknowledgment / Acuse:** Receipt confirmed within 24 hours
3. **Review / Revisión:** Independent review board assesses within 14 days
4. **Decision / Decisión:** Final decision communicated to all parties
5. **Implementation / Implementación:** If appeal successful, original decision modified or reversed

### Appeal Template / Plantilla de Apelación

```
TO: appeals@pranakaia.cl
SUBJECT: Appeal - [Original Case ID] - [Your Name]

1. ORIGINAL CASE REFERENCE: [Case ID/Date]

2. GROUNDS FOR APPEAL: [Select applicable]
   [ ] New evidence
   [ ] Process flaw
   [ ] Disproportionate decision
   [ ] Conflict of interest
   [ ] Other: ___________

3. DETAILED EXPLANATION:
   [Explain your grounds for appeal]

4. NEW EVIDENCE (if applicable):
   [Describe any new evidence and attach]

5. REQUESTED OUTCOME:
   [What resolution do you seek?]

6. CONTACT INFORMATION:
   [For communication about the appeal]
```
